## How it works

1. **Add your music.** Point Earport at the folder you already use, or import a playlist.
2. **Plug in a device.** Tell Earport what it is, and pick the playlists it should carry.
3. **Sync.** Earport copies what's new, converts what the device can't play, and removes only what it put there.

> [!NOTE]
> This is a beta. Keep a backup of any music you can't replace.

## Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Windows says "Windows protected your PC"</b></summary>
<br>

Earport isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It installs just for you, so no administrator password is needed, and it updates itself after that.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need Spotify?</b></summary>
<br>

No. A YouTube playlist link, a CSV file or an Apple Music export works too. Spotify import uses a free developer app you create yourself, which takes a few minutes and Earport walks you through it. Spotify requires that app's owner to have **Premium**, and each app works for up to 5 people.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What are the music tools?</b></summary>
<br>

yt-dlp, FFmpeg and Deno are separate, free programs. When you agree, Earport fetches them from their official releases and checks each download is genuine. They aren't part of Earport. Only download music you have the right to.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What does Earport send anywhere?</b></summary>
<br>

Nothing about you. It only connects to the services you use: Spotify when you import, YouTube when finding songs, MusicBrainz and cover-art sites when fixing song info, and GitHub for the music tools and updates. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Something went wrong</b></summary>
<br>

[Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) with your version (**Settings → About → Copy version details**) and what you were doing. Logs are in **Settings → Storage & cleanup → Advanced → Open logs folder**; check them for anything private before attaching.

For anything else, email **earport.support@gmail.com**.

</details>
